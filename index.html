AERARIUS

Your city. Your next chapter.

Aerarius is a city and real-estate tycoon game for iPhone and iPad. Start with a rented room, end up owning the skyline.

Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email adrianjahnsrud@live.no and say which device you play on.

Common questions

How do I remove ads? Tap the gear at the top right, then Ads → Remove ads forever. It is a one-time purchase.

I bought Remove Ads on another device. Open the same section and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.

Where is my progress saved? On your device, in one of three save slots. There is no account.

Can I change my ad privacy choices? Yes: gear → Ads → Privacy & ad choices.
