Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

Your game

Your saves, settings and progress are stored only on your device. Aerarius has no accounts and does not send your game to any server of ours.

Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking), approximate location derived from your IP address, ad interactions such as views and taps, and basic diagnostic and performance data.

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings → Ads → Privacy & ad choices. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Tracking

If you allow tracking when iOS asks, ads can be more relevant to you. If you decline, you still see ads, but less personalised ones. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Purchases

"Remove Ads" is bought through Apple. Payment details are handled by Apple and never reach us; the app only learns whether the purchase is active.

Children

Aerarius is not directed at children under 13, and ads are not intentionally targeted at them.
